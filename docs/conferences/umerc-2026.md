# UMERC 2026: Marine Energy Resource Characterization

This page collects every link from the Marine Energy Resource Characterization workshop presented at the [2026 UMERC + METS Conference](https://umerc-us.org/networks/events/154131)

Program overview: [Marine Energy Resource Assessment and Characterization](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/water/marine-energy-resource-assessment-and-characterization)

The session covered measurement campaigns, the data dissemination ecosystem (Marine Energy Atlas, MHKDR/OEDI, MHKiT), a live Atlas demonstration, the wave hindcast, and hands-on tidal exercises at Western Passage. Links are listed under the title of the slide they appear on, so the page can be followed alongside the presentation.
