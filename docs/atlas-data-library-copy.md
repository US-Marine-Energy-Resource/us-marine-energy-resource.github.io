## Long

*~140 words - "About this data" section, modal, data library page header*

The Marine Energy Atlas presents reconnaissance-level, aggregated summary data consisting of pre-computed spatial averages derived from larger physics-based hindcast simulations of tidal currents and ocean surface waves across the United States. This data is developed as part of the **U.S. Marine Energy Resource Characterization Project**, a collaboration between National Laboratory of the Rockies, Sandia National Laboratories, and Pacific Northwest National Laboratory, funded by the U.S. Department of Energy.

The Atlas is the right starting point for exploring where resources exist and comparing potential across regions. The project documentation goes further:

- **Variable definitions** - what each layer measures, units, and equations
- **Methodology** - how models were built, configured, and validated
- **Data limitations** - known caveats and appropriate use cases
- **Full dataset access** - complete time-series data via MHKDR, HSDS, and AWS S3
- **Citations and references** - for use in formal assessments

[Explore the full documentation →](https://us-marine-energy-resource.github.io/)
