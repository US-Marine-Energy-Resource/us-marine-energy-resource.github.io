NLR Marine Energy Atlas Data Library: UI Copy

U.S. Marine Energy Resource Characterization Project

Published

April 22, 2026

Executive Summary

The NLR Marine Energy Atlas maps reconnaissance-level, time-averaged hindcast summaries modeled and validated by PNNL, NLR, and Sandia National Laboratories. The Atlas is the right starting point for site comparison and resource screening, but does not provide the context needed to apply or cite the data. These copy snippets bridge that gap, pointing users to the project website where variable definitions, methodology, validation details, data access links, and references live. Three lengths for different UI placements, each in three tones. Option B (Trust-building) is recommended based on reviewer consensus.

For variable definitions, methodology, known limitations, and data access links, visit the project website.

Long

~140 words – “About this data” section, modal, data library page header

Option A: Explicit

The NLR Marine Energy Atlas maps reconnaissance-level, time-averaged summaries from physics-based hindcast simulations of tidal currents and ocean surface waves across the United States. The map is the right starting point for identifying where resources exist, but does not provide the context needed to understand, apply, or cite the data.

The U.S. Marine Energy Resource Characterization Project, a collaboration between Pacific Northwest National Laboratory, National Laboratory of the Rockies, and Sandia National Laboratories, funded by the U.S. Department of Energy’s Hydropower and Hydrokinetic Office, provides that context:

  • Variable definitions: units, equations, and what each layer measures
  • Methodology: how models were built, configured, and validated
  • Data limitations: known caveats and appropriate use cases
  • Data access links: 42-year wave hindcast (1979-2020) and tidal hindcast time-series data via MHKDR and AWS S3
  • References: citations for use in formal assessments

Option B: Trust-building (recommended)

The NLR Marine Energy Atlas maps reconnaissance-level, time-averaged summaries from physics-based hindcast simulations of tidal currents and ocean surface waves across the United States. The data was produced by Pacific Northwest National Laboratory, National Laboratory of the Rockies, and Sandia National Laboratories, funded by the U.S. Department of Energy’s Hydropower and Hydrokinetic Office, using validated numerical models built to recognized resource assessment standards.

The U.S. Marine Energy Resource Characterization Project documents everything needed to use this data with confidence:

  • Variable definitions: units, equations, and what each layer measures
  • Methodology: how models were built, configured, and validated
  • Data limitations: known caveats and appropriate use cases
  • Data access links: 42-year wave hindcast (1979-2020) and tidal hindcast time-series data via MHKDR and AWS S3
  • References: citations for use in formal assessments
